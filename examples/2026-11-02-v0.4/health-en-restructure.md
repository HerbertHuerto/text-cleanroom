In its 14 August 2024 statement, WHO reported that its Director-General, following independent expert advice, had declared the mpox upsurge in the Democratic Republic of the Congo and a growing number of African countries a public health emergency of international concern under the International Health Regulations (2005). Further spread across Africa, and possibly beyond, remained a risk rather than a certainty.

This was the second mpox emergency declaration of its kind in two years. The previous declaration, made in July 2022, had ended in May 2023 after a sustained global decline.

The initial anticipated funding requirement for the response was US$15 million; US$1.45 million had already been released from the Contingency Fund for Emergencies. The previous week, the vaccine Emergency Use Listing process had been initiated to accelerate access where national approval was missing and facilitate procurement by Gavi and UNICEF. Initiating the process did not mean that listing had been completed.
